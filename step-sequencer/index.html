Build a loop in your browser

A 16-step sequencer with five tracks and no samples. Every sound is synthesized live with WebAudio, so the whole instrument is one file. Tap the grid, press play, and you have a loop.

The sequencer

Presets:

Stopped. Tap a square to add a hit.

How it sounds

Nothing here is a recording. The kick is a sine wave dropping from 150 Hz to 40 Hz. The snare is a burst of noise plus a short tone. The hi-hat is highpassed noise that lasts 0.05 seconds. The bass is a sawtooth wave through a lowpass filter, and the pluck is a triangle wave with a fast decay. Each track plays a note from its own little scale, so the bass and pluck steps land on different pitches as the loop moves.

Drag across the grid to paint a row of hits, or erase them the same way. The presets fill the grid with a starting pattern you can change.

How it keeps time

The scheduler looks 0.1 seconds ahead and queues notes on the audio clock, not on the screen clock, so the beat stays steady even if the page is busy. The playhead is drawn separately from the audio, so a slow frame never drags the sound with it.

Try it: load the House preset, press play, then drag the tempo up to 128. Same loop, new energy.

Built with vanilla JS and WebAudio. No samples, no libraries, no build step.
